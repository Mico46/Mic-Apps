Privacy Policy

BalanceCheck · Last updated September 22, 2026

The short version: BalanceCheck reads your bank SMS messages on your device only. Nothing is uploaded — we have no servers, no accounts, and no way to see your data.

1. Who we are

BalanceCheck is a mobile application developed and published by Mic Apps ("we", "us", or "our"). This policy explains what information the app accesses, how it is used, and your choices.

2. Information the app accesses

To show your balances and transactions, the app requests access to:

  • SMS messages (Android). The app reads your inbox to find notifications from supported banks and wallets (CBE, Telebirr, BOA, Dashen/Amole, Awash, CBE Birr, M-Pesa, Apollo, Enat, Bunna, Tsehay, and ZamZam). Each message is parsed locally to extract the bank name, amount, credit/debit type, reference number, date, and current balance.
  • Manual entries. On iOS, or if you deny the SMS permission, you can type transactions in yourself. The SMS permission is optional — the app works without it.

The app does not access your contacts, location, photos, microphone, or camera.

3. How your information is used

  • Parsed transactions are stored in a local SQLite database on your device and are used only to display your balances, bank cards, and transaction history inside the app.
  • Settings such as which banks you have enabled and whether balances are hidden are stored locally (Android SharedPreferences).
  • Parsed data is never used for advertising, profiling, or analytics.

4. What is NOT collected or shared

  • We do not collect, transmit, or sell your personal data, SMS content, or transaction history.
  • We do not operate any server that receives your data — all processing happens on your phone.
  • We do not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or trackers.
  • No account or registration is required, so we never know who you are.
  • The only internet access the app makes is when you tap a link (such as this Privacy Policy or the Terms of Service), which opens in your browser.

5. Data storage and retention

  • All data lives on your device until you remove it.
  • Deleting the app removes its local database and settings.
  • You can clear all stored transactions from within the app at any time.
  • Because no copy exists off-device, deleted data cannot be recovered by us — it is simply gone.

6. Security

Your data is protected by the sandboxing and encryption features of your device's operating system. Since the data never leaves your phone, the main risks are physical access to an unlocked device — we recommend keeping your device lock (PIN/biometrics) enabled and using the app's show/hide balance toggle in public.

7. Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

8. Bank and telecom disclaimers

BalanceCheck is an independent tool and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any bank or telecom provider named in the app. Trademarks and brand names belong to their respective owners.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update it on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the revised policy.
